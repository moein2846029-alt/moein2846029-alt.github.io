About the MoeinX.Ai content tools

To manage our own accounts efficiently, we use internal tools that connect to the official APIs of YouTube, Instagram and TikTok (including TikTok Login Kit). These tools are used only by the owner of MoeinX.Ai to review the performance of our own content and to prepare and publish it. They are not offered to the public, and they do not collect data from other people. Details are in our Privacy Policy.
