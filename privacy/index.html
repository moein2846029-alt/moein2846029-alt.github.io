Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how MoeinX.Ai (“we”, “us”) handles information on this website and in the internal tools we use to manage our own YouTube, Instagram and TikTok accounts.

1. Who we are

MoeinX.Ai is an independent creative project run by an individual creator. You can contact us through Instagram (@moeinx.ai) or through our YouTube channel (@MoeinX_Ai).

2. This website

This website is a static site hosted on GitHub Pages. We do not run analytics or advertising scripts, do not set cookies of our own, and do not ask visitors for personal information. Our hosting provider may process technical data such as IP addresses in server logs, according to its own privacy policy.

3. Data accessed through TikTok, YouTube and Instagram

We use the official APIs of these platforms, including TikTok Login Kit, only to connect accounts that we own. When an account is authorized, we may receive an access token and the permissions (scopes) that were granted, such as basic profile information (display name and avatar), information about our own videos and posts and, where granted, the ability to upload content to our own account.

4. How we use this data

The data is used only to review the performance of our own content and to prepare and publish our own content. We do not sell this data, do not share it with advertisers, and do not use it to profile other people.

5. Storage and service providers

Access tokens are stored in encrypted form by an authentication service provider (Composio) that acts on our behalf. We do not store passwords of these platforms. Apart from such service providers, and where required by law, we do not disclose this data to third parties.

6. Retention, revocation and deletion

We keep access tokens only as long as the connection is needed. You can revoke access at any time in the settings of the platform concerned (for TikTok, in the connected apps or permissions section of your account settings). To ask us to delete any data connected with you, contact us through the channels above and we will respond within a reasonable time.

7. Children

Our website and tools are not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
